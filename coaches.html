Coaching Philosophy

We coach fundamentals first: stance, motion, and position before technique-of-the-week. Every wrestler gets attention, regardless of size, athleticism, or experience level.

Practice Expectations

  • Arrive on time, dressed and ready to move
  • Full effort during drilling — technique comes from reps
  • Respect for coaches, teammates, and the mat
  • Water breaks as needed — hydration matters

Basic Wrestling Terminology

Takedown2 pts
Escape1 pt

Near Fall2–3 pts

Home Drills

Simple stance-and-motion, sprawls, and shot drilling for 10–15 minutes a few times a week reinforces what's covered at practice. Ask your coach for age-appropriate drill suggestions.

Tournament Coaching Expectations

Coaches will be mat-side at every Round Robin to corner wrestlers, call out reminders, and support in bracket and score questions. Parents are asked to stay off the mat and let coaches lead during matches.

Cardinal Wrestling Fundamentals

Our structured technique curriculum — stance and motion, basic takedowns, escapes, and control positions — builds progressively across the season so wrestlers keep advancing year over year, not starting from scratch each fall.
