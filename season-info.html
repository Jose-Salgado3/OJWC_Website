Season Info

This is your parent reference page — everything you need is here, so you never have to dig through Facebook Messenger for it.

What Wrestlers Need to Bring

  • Shorts/Sweats & T-shirt
    • No zippers allowed on Mats
  • Wrestling shoes
    • For mat use only
  • Headgear
    • Optional at Practice, but Recommended. Required at Tournaments.
  • Mouthguard
    • Required for braces
  • Water bottle

Equipment Requirements

All wrestlers must have proper wrestling shoes and headgear for practice and competition. Loaner gear may be available for first-time families - ask a coach or message us.

Skin & Nail Requirements

USA Wrestling requires short, trimmed nails and skin free of rashes or communicable conditions. Wrestlers who don't pass a skin check may be held out of competition - plan ahead and check the night before Practice/Competition. If you find a possible communicable condition, see a doctor and have them fill out the Skin Lesion Form 2026–2027 that can be found in our Document Library on our website. A doctor's signature is required with a date they can return to participation - prior to being eligible to practice or compete again.

Weigh-In Information

Weigh-ins happen the morning of each Round Robin, ahead of the posted "Wrestling Begins" time. Arrive early - specific weigh-in windows are listed on each tournament event's flyer.

Varsity / JV Information

Varsity and Junior Varsity positions are earned through our team wrestle-off process. Wrestlers compete within their applicable age & weight class, with wrestle-off results determining Varsity, JV, and Exhibition placement. The top wrestler in each weight class earns the Varsity position, followed by Junior Varsity and, when applicable, Exhibition wrestlers. This process gives every wrestler a fair opportunity to earn their spot through competition on the mat. Varsity and JV wrestlers represent Orting in the PCJWL postseason tournament series. Additional details, including wrestle-off format, scheduling, and eligibility, will be communicated by the coaching staff during the season, and a schedule for Wrestle-offs will be shared.

Tournament Expectations

Arrive on time! Bring all Wrestling gear - Singlet, Wrestling Shoes, Headgear, Team Warm-Ups (Optional). Tournament day volunteer assignments are shared in advance — every Team is expected to help cover at least one Table (mat) per tournament (see Volunteer Info).

FAQs

Do I need experience to sign up? No — all skill levels are welcome, especially beginners.

What if we don't have gear yet? Contact a coach — we can often help with loaner shoes or headgear for the first few weeks.
