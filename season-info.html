Season Info

This is your parent reference page — everything you need is here, so you never have to dig through Facebook Messenger for it.

What Wrestlers Need to Bring

  • Club singlet and practice clothes
  • Wrestling shoes
  • Headgear
  • Water bottle
  • Mouthguard (varsity/JV level)

Equipment Requirements

All wrestlers must have proper wrestling shoes and headgear for practice and competition. Loaner gear may be available for first-time families — ask a coach.

Skin & Nail Requirements

USA Wrestling requires short, trimmed nails and skin free of rashes or communicable conditions for weigh-ins. Wrestlers who don't pass a skin check may be held out of competition — plan ahead and check the night before.

Weigh-In Information

Weigh-ins happen the morning of each Round Robin, ahead of the posted "Wrestling Begins" time. Arrive early — specific weigh-in windows are listed on each event's Schedule entry.

Varsity / JV Information

Wrestlers are placed in varsity or JV groupings based on age, weight, and experience per PCJWL guidelines. Your coach will confirm placement ahead of the first Round Robin.

Tournament Expectations

Arrive on time, in uniform, with all required gear. Tournament day volunteer assignments are shared in advance — every family is expected to help cover at least one role per season (see Parents & Volunteers).

FAQs

Do I need experience to sign up? No — all skill levels are welcome, especially beginners.

What if we don't have gear yet? Contact a coach — we can often help with loaner shoes or headgear for the first few weeks.
