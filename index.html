Welcome to Cardinal Wrestling

Orting Junior Cardinals Wrestling is a nonprofit youth wrestling program serving Orting kids ages 5–12, proudly affiliated with the Puget Sound Junior Wrestling League (PCJWL). Building wrestlers. Building character. Building Cardinals. Whether your child has never stepped on a mat or is training for their next tournament, there's a place for them here.
